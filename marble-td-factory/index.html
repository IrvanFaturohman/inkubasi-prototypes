What changed in v2

1Towers never go silent. Each one has a weak auto-attack that runs on nothing
2Matching colour = combo +1, and combo multiplies damage and rate
3×3 POWER · ×5 SUPER · ×8 MEGA each fire a one-off spectacle the moment you cross them
4Wrong colour is converted to energy, not rejected — the combo survives
5The belt cannot jam. A crowded belt charges OVERLOAD instead
6OVERLOAD at 100% = +3 combo on every tower, screen-wide blast, 5s of fast flow
7Enemy weakness is a ×1.8 bonus, not a lock — every tower hurts every enemy

The rhythm it is built around

Calm flow → you catch three matching marbles → POWER fires → belt gets busy → OVERLOAD charges → MEGA plus overload land together → screen clears.

MEGA spends the combo back to zero on purpose. The payoff is the reset, and the reset is what makes you want the next one.

Congestion is now a battery

Letting marbles pile up used to end the run. Now the OVERLOAD bar fills faster the fuller the belt gets, and dumping the pile down SCRAP feeds it too.

So there is a real, positive choice: spend marbles on a combo now, or hoard the belt and cash the whole thing in at once.

What skill still buys you

A beginner survives and lands ×3s. A good player times the gate to catch three of the same colour in a row, and holds the belt full so OVERLOAD lands on top of a MEGA.

The gap between them is small combos vs huge combos — not survive vs lose.

Honest limits

Prototype only — no meta, economy, IAP or ads. Audio is synthesised in code. Balance is hand-tuned by feel and has not been playtested; the tier thresholds and multipliers are the first numbers to move.

v2 answers a different question from v1: not “can you avoid failure?” but “how big a combo can you build?” Same factory, same gates, same waves — the punishment layer was removed and a reward layer put in its place. Feel targets: chunky 3D, ASMR audio, readable silhouettes. Marble hues validated for colour-blind separation (worst pair ΔE 18.9 deutan).
