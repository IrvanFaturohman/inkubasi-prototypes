Where the decision lives

The belt is the preview — you can see what is coming. The dilemma is committing a tray early versus keeping one open for a colour you can see arriving in four slots.

With three colours and twelve tray slots, “no clean placement” happens often. That is the point: you choose which tray to sacrifice.

Where the money sits

A jammed tray is a self-inflicted, visible, recoverable loss — the strongest possible setup for a booster sale. The hammer clears one.

Pixel Flow ships exactly this at L230: Wooden Blocks and Hammers, which the deconstruction describes as forcing tool prioritisation.

Why 3D, specifically

This is Section 5 rule 1 — the Voodoo-Grand hybridisation: an old hyper-casual verb rebuilt with 3D models, weighted motion and satisfying settle.

Pieces carry shape and colour: cube, cylinder, octahedron. Three hues is the most that clears colour-blind separation, so silhouette does the rest.
