The 9 things doing the work

  1. Rising pitch — each piece in a tray plays the next note up a pentatonic scale
  2. Squash & stretch on launch and landing
  3. Gravity arc, not a linear tween
  4. Hitstop — 90ms freeze on every clear
  5. Screen shake, scaled to the event
  6. Particle burst in the piece's own colour
  7. Elastic settle — pieces overshoot and wobble
  8. Combo escalation — pitch, shake and score all climb
  9. Haptics via vibrate on mobile

Why pitch is the big one

A tray filling up plays do-re-mi-so as it goes. Your ear tracks progress before your eyes do, and an unfinished tray becomes an unresolved chord — you want to finish it.

That is the actual mechanism under “ASMR sorting”. It is not decoration on the loop, it is a second feedback channel running in parallel with the visuals.

Still honest about this

Everything here is synthesised in code — no audio files, no artist. A shipped game replaces every one of these with recorded foley, real materials and a tuned soft-body settle.

This is the floor of acceptable feel, not the ceiling. If it already reads as satisfying at this fidelity, the mechanic is worth funding.

Feel targets: Marble Sort (ASMR marble physics), Chirpy Sort (ASMR bird audio), Sand Loop (relaxation-led).
